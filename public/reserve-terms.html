Signature Reserve Terms

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What you’re reserving

Signature Reserve is a 12-month credit balance with Steel & Zane, bought as a Listing Tier.

  • Your credit is a dollar amount. Each Listing Tier equals the estimated number of listings × $845. A 6 Listing Tier is $5,070 of credit, whichever payment option you choose.
  • Listings are an estimate. Each shoot is deducted from your credit at the Signature rate for that property’s square footage, plus any add-ons you book. You may complete more or fewer listings than your tier’s number.
  • Your reservation runs 12 months from the date of your first payment.
  • Your full credit is added to your Steel & Zane account once your first payment goes through.

Payment

You pay in full or on a monthly plan, as shown at checkout. Paying up front earns a lower price for the same credit.

  • Monthly plans end on their own after the final scheduled payment. No further charges follow.
  • Your full credit is available after your first payment. Because of this, all remaining scheduled payments stay due and cannot be cancelled early, whether or not you have used your credit.
  • If a scheduled payment fails, new bookings pause until your card is updated. Shoots already booked go ahead.

Price lock

The Signature rates per square foot presented to you when you reserve are locked for your 12-month reservation. If our prices go up during that time, your shoots are still deducted at your locked rates.

Signature Guarantee

Every Signature Reserve client gets the Signature Guarantee for the full reservation.

  1. Next-business-day delivery. Photos are delivered the next business day after the shoot, Saturdays included, to our quality standard. Delivery is by the end of that day; we do not promise a specific time. If we deliver late, that shoot is not deducted from your credit.
  2. Edits at no cost. If a delivered photo needs editing to meet our quality standard, we handle it at no charge.
  3. The home must be photo-ready. Photo-ready means clean, decluttered and staged for photography. Steel & Zane decides whether a home is photo-ready on shoot day. If it is not, you can reschedule, or the shoot can go ahead. A shoot that goes ahead is delivered as captured, and the no-cost edits in point 2 do not apply to that shoot.

Unused credit

At the end of your 12-month reservation, any unused credit either carries over or is refunded. You choose.

  • Carry it over: your unused credit moves into the next 12 months. Credit never expires.
  • Get a refund: we refund the unused share of what you actually paid, to your original payment method. Refunds can only be requested at the end of the reservation, within 30 days after it ends.

The refund is the share of your credit left unused, applied to the amount you paid:

Refund = (Unused credit ÷ Total credit) × Amount paid

Example: on a 6 Listing Tier paid in full, you pay $4,056 for $5,070 of credit. If $2,535 of credit is unused at the end (half), your refund is half of $4,056, which is $2,028.
