Privacy Policy

Last updated: [CONFIRM: effective date]

Steel & Zane (“we,” “us”) provides real estate photography and media services in the Los Angeles area. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you request a shoot or contact us, we collect what you give us:

  • Your name, cell phone number and email address
  • Details about your listing, such as its address, whether it is active or coming soon, and your preferred shoot date
  • Anything else you choose to tell us in a notes field or message

We save your answers as you move through our request form, so we may receive your information even if you don’t finish every step.

We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as the pages you visit, the referring link and campaign codes in the link you used, your browser and device type, and the date and time of your visit.

How we use it

  • To contact you by phone, text message or email about your shoot and your listing
  • To schedule and deliver our services
  • To understand how our website and outreach are performing, so we can improve them
  • To protect our website against spam and abuse

Text messages

If you give us your cell number, you agree that we may contact you by call or text about your shoot. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text-messaging consent with third parties for their own marketing.

We do not sell your personal information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run our business, and they process information only on our behalf. These include website hosting (Vercel), email and document storage (Google Workspace), website analytics (Google Analytics), address lookup as you type a listing address (Google Maps Platform), video hosting (Vidalytics) and our booking and order platform (Spiro).

Cookies and analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies to measure visits and how pages are used. Our video player may also use cookies to measure viewing. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on. Our request form also stores your in-progress answers on your own device so you can pick up where you left off; this is cleared when you finish.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
